Cookies are a way to store state data for a client on a web site. Cookies come from [[HTTP Response]]s and are part of the [[Client Server Architecture]]

Cookies can for example be used for
- Authorization
- Shopping carts
- Recommendations
- User Session State

Cookies permit the sites to learn a lot about the user, privacy issues
Third party persistent cookies are a big problem for integrity
Third party cookies may come from ads 
Disabled by default on Firefox and other modern web browsers
Web extensions can help protect from third party cookies.
GDPR helps defend against malignant cookies
