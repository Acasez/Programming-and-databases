Video remains the dominant source of residential downstream internet traffic

Different users have different capabilities (e.g., wired versus mobile; bandwidth rich versus bandwidth poor)

Use coding to decrease bits used encode images. 
* spatial (within image)
	• Reducing the bits sent in an image
• temporal (from one image to next)
	• Reducing the bits sent between image

[[DASH]] is one form of streaming, [[CDN]] is another.
