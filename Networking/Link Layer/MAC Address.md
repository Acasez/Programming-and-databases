A form of [[Network Address|network address]] used on the [[Link Layer|link layer]] and for [[Network Layer|network]] forwarding.

Permanent address, burnt into the cards. 
48 bit stored in in hexadecimals

Analogy:
- MAC address - Personal number
- IP address - Postal address
