A challenge for [[Cellular Networks|cellular networks]]. Devices that move outside of their home networks are [[Roaming]].

Can't let routers handle it, not scalable. Let the edge of the network handle it
Indirect routing - Goes to home network then remote/current location
Direct routing - Gets it directly.

WIFI networks have no notation of home or foreign networks.
