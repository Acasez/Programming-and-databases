Used by [[Wireless Networks]], don't need wires (obviously) but have other challenges.

## Wireless Links vs Wired Links
Decreased signal strength over time as it travels
Interferences from other sources. 2.4 GHz shared by many devices.
Multipath Propagation - Can't predict exact path of wireless links

SNR, Signal to noise ratio - Large SNR is good, less noise compared to noise.
BER, Bit Error Rate - The proportion of received bits that differs from transmitted bits. 

SNR versus BER tradeoffs

Multiple Wireless senders and receivers

## CDMA
Code Division Multiple Access. 
Used in 3G Cellular networks
All nodes transfer at the same frequency 
All users have their own chipping sequence code to encode data.
Receivers decode data based on the chipping sequence
