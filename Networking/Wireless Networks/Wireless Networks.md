Laptops, smartphones, IoT. May be stationary or mobile, not just smartphones. Connects to [[The Internet]] over [[Wireless Links|wireless links]]

[[WIFI]], Bluetooth & [[Cellular Networks|cellular networks]].

Backend is wired. 
Wireless networks connect to base stations that connect to backend.
Wireless links are the connections between hosts and base stations. 

No standard for distance and capacity over wireless links

## Wireless networks modes

### Infrastructure mode
Base stations connect to, sends you between base stations as you move
### Ad Hoc Mode
Not centralized, connect to nearest
