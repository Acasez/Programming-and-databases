Connection points to a [[Wireless Networks]] that connect to the wider internet. WIFI for example.

New users have to connect and authenticate with the access points to connect.

Can connect via passive scanning or active scanning.
![[Pasted image 20261103093547.png]]

Wireless connections all you a random access style [[Multiple Access Protocol]] to connect.
Can try to avoid collisions by sending a small RTS (request to send) packet to claim a spot at the access point and then send their message. RTS packets are so small that collisions don't matter as much.
