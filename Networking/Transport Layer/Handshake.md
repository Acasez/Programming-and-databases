The "Handshake" is a step in the connection management used by [[TCP]]. Handshakes are used to confirm a network connection and agree on connection parameters. 

Default handshakes are 2-way
Lets talk --> Okay

Problems might occur
- variable delays
- retransmitted messages
- message reordering
- can't see the other side


3-way handshake, used by [[TCP]].
