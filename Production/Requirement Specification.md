A requirement specification is the overall use cases and functionality desired by the client for an application.

They are often broken down into [[User Stories]] during the [[Design and Planning]] phase of development. The use cases can and should often be measured in order of importance, scope and risk to plan out which tasks to prioritize for the project. 

For a good project the requirements need to be clear and the architecture and frameworks need to be selected based on them.
